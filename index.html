Import Raw Image

Drag the corner handles (TL, TR, BR, BL) to the outer corners of the QR code, without the quiet zone. Mouse wheel zooms, drag an empty area to pan when zoomed in. Arrow keys nudge the last moved handle (Shift for 10px). Each module is sampled at its center: dark becomes black, light becomes white and anything in between becomes grey (unknown), so damaged areas can be reconstructed later.

Enhance & Decode Image

Clean up a photo before decoding. Drag on the image to select (crop) only the QR code, then adjust rotation, scale, contrast and threshold until it decodes. Mouse wheel over the image also zooms the crop. When the status turns green the QR code was detected and can be imported; otherwise send the cleaned image to Raw Image import to reconstruct it by hand.
